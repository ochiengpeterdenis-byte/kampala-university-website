Who We Are

Welcome to Kampala University. This page introduces our university, its learning community and its commitment to education, knowledge and service. Explore our academic programmes and contact the university for further information.

Our Mission

To support learning, research and the development of knowledge and practical skills.

Our Vision

To pursue excellence in education and contribute positively to society.

Our Values

Excellence, integrity, service, respect and a commitment to lifelong learning.

What We Aim to Do

  • Provide opportunities for academic and personal development.
  • Encourage research, creativity and independent learning.
  • Help students develop knowledge and practical skills.
  • Promote responsible service to the wider community.
