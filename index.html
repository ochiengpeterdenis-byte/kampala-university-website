Welcome to Kampala University

Kampala University is a private chartered institution of higher learning in Uganda, established in 1998.

The university is committed to quality education, research, innovation and community development.

Who We Are

Kampala University is a higher learning institution in Uganda. This website provides information about the university, its academic programmes, admissions and other services.

Our website aims to make it easier for prospective students, current students, parents and other visitors to access important university information.

Our Vision

To promote excellence in education, research, innovation and community development.

Our Mission

To provide quality education and develop knowledgeable, skilled and responsible graduates.

Our Values

Excellence, integrity, innovation, discipline, professionalism and social responsibility.

Our Educational Objectives

  • Promote quality higher education.
  • Encourage research and innovation.
  • Develop practical professional skills.
  • Promote responsible leadership.
  • Contribute to community development.
